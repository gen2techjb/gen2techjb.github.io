PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

Switchboards & control panels

Our product range is organised into three core categories.

MSB / MCC

Main Switchboard / Motor Control Centre

The MSB is the central hub of a power distribution system, controlling and distributing electricity across multiple circuits. The MCC centrally manages and protects electric motors for improved reliability.

SSB / DB

Sub Switchboard / Distribution Board

The SSB is a secondary distribution point, extending power from the main switchboard to specific areas, while the DB provides localised distribution within a designated space.

Control Panel

Control Panels

A planned range of control panels including transfer pump panels, telemonitoring system panels, and wet riser fire pump panels.
